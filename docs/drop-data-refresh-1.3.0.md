# Drop-data review for 1.3.0

Checked on 22 October 2026 against a freshly fetched OSRS Wiki Bucket API snapshot and RuneLite's current public item-name dump. The comparison baseline is the Plugin Hub's published 1.2.0 commit, `6844094eea14164f43eea4214273437fd6e93891`.

## Applied changes

- `droprates_clean.json`: 52 added source/item rows, 38 changed rows, one precision-only adjustment and six removed rows. These are row counts, not counts of newly released items.
- `npc_versions.json`: ten added source/version/item rows.
- `rare_drop_table.json`: 190 changed rows, including repeated source variants and revised roll multipliers.
- `rdt_quantities.json`: unchanged.

Examples include a newly listed approximate Frozen tear chance for Cave crawler, additional Energy potion drops and revised Killerwatt chances. Approximate rates retain their approximation marker.

The hand-maintained special and minigame tables were also compared where their entries could be matched to a numerical fraction in the fresh Bucket rows. No probability differences were found in the 103 directly comparable entries. Some derived rates and differently named reward sources were not directly comparable; this is not a claim that every special reward probability was independently recalculated. Clue rates were not regenerated by the NPC crawler.

## New items and conditional reward tables

Loot Pop obtains item definitions, names and sprites through RuneLite rather than maintaining an item whitelist. A new item does not need a manually added ID to be displayed when RuneLite reports its loot event. Its drop chance is a separate lookup; an absent chance stays unknown.

The latest item-name dump includes the Fractured Archive rewards, including Horn of Bran, Ascension crossbows, Rondache, Obligator and Zorya's tome variants. This name check is not an in-game end-to-end test of the raid's loot event.

The [Fractured Archive chest page](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Chest_(The_Fractured_Archive)) explicitly says its unique-item fractions are conditional on a unique having dropped. They are not per-raid probabilities, and the mechanics of obtaining a unique are not yet known. Those weights were not added as unconditional chances or rarity classifications. The NPC crawler intentionally does not import arbitrary reward-chest tables.

## Validation

The generator validated the refreshed rate strings. Four added regression tests cover newly listed drops, NPC-specific variants, fractional rare-table rolls and removal of obsolete rates. All 129 automated tests pass, including a Journal Gson-configuration compatibility test, and the 1.3.0 JAR builds successfully. No runtime network requests or image downloads were added.

The detailed pre-update comparison and raw download cache are local build artifacts under `build/` and are not part of the plugin JAR.
